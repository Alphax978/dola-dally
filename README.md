# dola-daily


## Video sources
- **YouMind**: public sitemaps and listing pages.
- **ReelCine** (reelcine.org/video-prompt/N): automated access authorized by ReelCine support in October 2026 (per the account owner). Requests are kept low: at most ~80 entry pages a day, 1 second apart.
- **Domer** (domer.io/prompts/seedance-N, from its public sitemap): automated access permitted by Domer support in October 2026 (per the account owner). At most ~40 entry pages a day, 1 second apart.
- Sources rotate by day (one goes first, the others fill gaps). Videos are de-duplicated across sources by their original X post link.
