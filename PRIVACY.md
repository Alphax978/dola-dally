# Privacy Policy — dola-daily

Last updated: 17 October 2026

dola-daily is a personal automation that posts AI videos to the owner's own social media accounts (X, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok).

**What it accesses:** only the owner's own accounts, through the official APIs, with permission to upload and publish videos and captions.

**What it stores:** access tokens are kept as encrypted GitHub Secrets. No personal data from other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

**Who uses it:** only the account owner. It is not offered to the public.

**Removing access:** you can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google/YouTube) or in your Meta, X and TikTok app settings.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/Alphax978/dola-dally/issues
