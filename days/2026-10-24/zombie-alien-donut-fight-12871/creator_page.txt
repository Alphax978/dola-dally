Rain streaking the front window, a red traffic light outside. 0:00-0:03 THE FREEZE Handheld medium down the counter. A customer drops a coffee cup, coffee arcing. The zombie and the alien lunge at the case from opposite ends. Sound drops out. Everything stops: coffee hangs as a glossy brown ribbon, raindrops pinned on the glass, the cashier's yawn locked open. Only the two leads keep moving. 0:03-0:08 REALIZATION Both straighten, look at the hanging coffee, then each other. Slow 30 degree orbit. ALIEN: "I have crossed four galaxies for this donut." ALIEN: "That is not the same thing." 0:08-0:13 FIRST EXCHANGE The zombie swings a long stiff arm, the alien ducks, slides on wet tile and kicks a stool into his shins. He staggers, then pulls a folded umbrella from a frozen customer with real effort, the customer staying rigid. ZOMBIE: "Rude." 0:18-0:23 COUNTER FIGHT Fast tracking shot. She leaps stool to stool over frozen shoulders, he swings the umbrella, she parries with a frozen customer's rolled newspaper. Three crisp readable exchanges. His clip-on tie pops off. Her hood flops over her eyes. 0:23-0:27 TRUCE Both stop, breathing hard, hands on knees, coffee ribbon between them. ALIEN: "Share?" ZOMBIE: "Share."
